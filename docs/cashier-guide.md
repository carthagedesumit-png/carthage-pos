# CBOS Cashier Guide

Sign in with your assigned cashier account and store. Scan barcodes or search products, confirm quantities, select a customer when required, and verify tender totals before checkout. Mixed payments must add up to the amount due.

Use Suspend when a customer needs to pause. Resume from the hold queue. Returns and discounts require manager authorization. At shift end, count the drawer and complete cash reconciliation. Report printer, drawer, or customer-display errors without retrying a completed sale.
