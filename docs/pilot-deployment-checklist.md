# CBOS Pilot Deployment Checklist

- Run installer preflight and fresh installation.
- Complete company, store, administrator, tax, currency, receipt, printer, hours, backup, account, and license onboarding.
- Choose either an empty real company or the explicitly isolated demo company.
- Verify login, product creation, goods receipt, mixed sale, return, finance posting, and reports.
- Create and verify a backup; rehearse restore in an isolated directory.
- Restart CBOS and confirm browser launch, persistence, diagnostics, and unfinished-cart recovery.
- Confirm printer, cash drawer, customer display, network binding, firewall, disk space, and support contacts.
- Record pilot approval and rollback owner.
